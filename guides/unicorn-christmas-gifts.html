Christmas buying checklist

  1. Match the gift to the recipient's age and routine.
  2. Confirm the exact selected product, seller and quantity.
  3. Check dimensions before buying room decor.
  4. Review delivery dates and return terms before the holiday deadline.
  5. For a smaller present, use the stocking-stuffer guide; for a bigger room present, use the night-light guide.
