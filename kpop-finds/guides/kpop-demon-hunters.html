What to verify before buying

  • Whether the listing explicitly identifies the item as official/licensed merchandise.
  • The exact album/version/member selection instead of relying on the first image.
  • For light sticks: generation/version, app or concert compatibility, batteries and included accessories.
  • For clothing: size chart and seller/return terms.
  • For photocards and collectibles: whether the item is official, fan-made or a reproduction.

Avoid the most common mistake

Do not pay an official-merch premium just because a title contains “official.” Marketplace titles can be inaccurate. Cross-check packaging, product naming and seller information against an official source.
