Privacy

What happens when you visit K‑Pop Finds or follow a product link.

On this site

This prototype does not have user accounts or first-party analytics. The merch finder runs in your browser. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to serve the site.

Advertising

The site is prepared for Google AdSense, but the ad loader is disabled until a real approved publisher ID and ad-unit IDs are configured. If AdSense is enabled later, Google and advertising partners may use cookies or other local storage and process data to deliver, measure and protect advertising. A suitable consent management platform should be used wherever consent is required.

Affiliate links

Some Amazon links are paid links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon and other linked stores process visits and purchases under their own privacy notices. K‑Pop Finds does not receive your payment details.

Independent site

Artist names and brand names are used to identify the fandoms and merchandise discussed. K‑Pop Finds is not an official store and does not claim affiliation with the artists, labels, Netflix or retailers named in the guides.
