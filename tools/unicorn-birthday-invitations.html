What to put on a unicorn party invitation

A useful invitation makes the practical details easy to find. Include the full date, a start and finish time, the venue address, and a way to reply. If parents should stay, or if the party is a drop-off event, say so in the extra note.

Copy this invitation wording

You're invited to a magical unicorn birthday party! Join us on [date] from [start] to [finish] at [place]. Please RSVP to [host] by [deadline], and let us know about any dietary needs.

For a small gathering, you can replace the birthday line with “Join us for a unicorn afternoon.” Keep the invitation brief; send directions and any longer instructions separately.

Printing and sharing your invitation

  1. Fill in the form and check the preview. Fields left empty become writing lines.
  2. Choose Print / save PDF. The print layout includes only the invitation.
  3. Print one test page first. Use normal paper or cardstock that your printer supports.
  4. For a digital invitation, choose Save as PDF and send the file privately to your guests. This tool does not send invitations for you.

Only share a version containing an address or contact details with your intended guests. Free for personal party use.

Invitation questions

Can I make an invitation without entering personal details?

Yes. Leave the fields blank and print a template to fill in by hand.

Can I print several invitations?

Yes. Choose the number of copies in your printer's dialog. Each copy uses one page.

Will my invitation be saved?

No. The tool does not save your entries or put them in the page address. Save your finished invitation as a PDF before closing or reloading the page.
