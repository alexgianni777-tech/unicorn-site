Unicorn treasure hunt

Help your unicorn find six magical symbols. Print the hiding cards and a matching sheet, then turn your party space into a treasure trail.

Get the free printablesHow to play

A party game you can set up in minutes

Suggested ages: 4–8 with an adult helper. Suggested play time: 10–15 minutes. Play together as one team or print a matching sheet for each child. The symbols work in black and white; children can match pictures without reading.

  1. Print one set of the six hiding cards. An adult cuts along the dotted borders.
  2. Place the cards in six easy-to-reach spots within your agreed play area. Keep them visible enough to find without climbing or opening cupboards.
  3. Give each child or team a matching sheet and a pencil. Leave the hiding cards in place so everyone can find them.
  4. When a player spots a symbol, they tick the matching picture. Find all six to complete the quest.
  5. Gather everyone to count the symbols and celebrate together. A prize is optional.

Make it easier: use one room and place cards in plain sight. Make it harder: spread the cards across a larger supervised area and give a warm-or-cold hint if someone gets stuck.

Print your treasure hunt

The full set has two pages: six cut-out cards on the first and one player sheet on the second. Print extra copies of the player sheet for more children. Use A4 or Letter paper, portrait orientation, and turn off browser headers and footers for a cleaner result.

You can also choose “Save as PDF” in your print menu. Free for personal party use. No sign-up required.

What you need — and optional party extras

Paper, a printer and pencils are enough to play. Tape can help display the cards; check the surface first. You do not need a purchased game kit.
