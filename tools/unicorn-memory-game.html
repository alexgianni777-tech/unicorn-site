How to play

  1. Turn over any two different cards. That counts as one move.
  2. If the pictures match, they stay face up.
  3. If they do not match, study them, then choose “Turn these cards back” when you are ready.
  4. Find all six pairs to finish. “New game” shuffles the cards and resets your moves.

The game does not save your score, ask for a name or send your moves to us. Reloading starts a fresh game.
